## What does chadlite mean

Chadlite sits one tier below Chad on the PSL rating scale, typically corresponding to a score around 7 to 7.9 out of 10. Someone at this level has strong facial features overall, such as a defined jawline, decent cheekbones, and reasonable facial harmony, but may have one or two areas holding them back. Common limiting factors include a weaker chin, slightly hooded rather than full hunter eyes, or suboptimal frame.

The "lite" suffix signals proximity to the top tier without full entry. A chadlite tends to receive noticeably positive social and dating outcomes compared to average-looking men, but the PSL community draws a clear distinction between this level and a true Chad, who scores 8 or above and typically has near-ideal scores across multiple facial categories.

## Origin

The term emerged from PSL (Pretty, Slayer, Lookism) online communities that developed detailed rating frameworks for male attractiveness. As those communities built out tiered hierarchies, chadlite filled the gap between high-tier normie and Chad, giving a more precise label to men who are clearly attractive but not at the upper extreme of the scale.
