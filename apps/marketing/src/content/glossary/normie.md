## What does normie mean

In looksmaxxing and PSL communities, a normie is someone whose facial attractiveness sits in the middle of the rating scale, roughly between 4 and 7 out of 10. They are not considered particularly attractive or unattractive by most observers. The term is borrowed from broader internet slang, where it originally described someone with mainstream tastes, but in this context it refers strictly to physical appearance.

The normie range is broken into three sub-levels. A low tier normie (LTN) sits near the bottom of the average band, around a 4 to 4.9. A mid tier normie (MTN) lands around a 5 to 5.9, and a high tier normie (HTN) reaches the upper average range, roughly 6 to 6.9. These distinctions matter in looksmaxxing because someone near the top of the normie range may only need minor improvements to move into above-average territory, while someone at the low end may benefit from more focused work.

## Origin

The tiered normie framework grew out of PSL rating culture, which adapted the 1-to-10 attractiveness scale into a more detailed taxonomy. Splitting the normie range into LTN, MTN, and HTN gave people a more granular way to describe where they stood and what kind of progress was realistic.
