## What does sub5 / sub3 mean

In looksmaxxing and PSL communities, attractiveness is commonly rated on a 1 to 10 scale. A "sub5" is anyone scoring below 5, meaning below the statistical average for their sex. A "sub3" is a more extreme designation, indicating a score below 3, which the community considers significantly below average. These labels are shorthand for positioning someone on that scale without assigning a precise decimal.

The sub5 / sub3 framework is used descriptively, not as a fixed biological verdict. Factors like grooming, body composition, posture, and skin quality all influence where someone lands on the scale, and many of these are addressable. The ratings are also subjective and context-dependent, varying by rater, cultural background, and the specific traits being evaluated.

Sub5 does not mean "unfixable." A significant portion of looksmaxxing content is specifically aimed at people who identify as sub5 and want to move toward average or above-average ratings through soft and hard improvements.

## Origin

The sub-N rating format comes from PSL (Psych, Sluthate, Lookism) online communities that developed numerical attractiveness scales as a pseudo-objective way to discuss facial aesthetics. Prefixing "sub" before a number to indicate falling below that threshold became standard shorthand across these spaces and eventually spread into broader looksmaxxing discourse.

## How it relates to looksmaxxing

For people who identify as sub5, looksmaxxing offers a structured path to improvement. Soft approaches, including better grooming, haircut optimization, fat loss, and skincare, can shift a rating noticeably. Hard approaches such as mewing, jaw exercises, or in some cases surgical consultation target underlying structural traits.

If you want an honest baseline, tools like the Aura app provide AI-driven PSL scoring and facial analysis, covering traits like jawline definition and eye area, along with a personalized improvement plan. Knowing where you actually stand is more useful than guessing. From there, the sub5 label becomes a starting point rather than a ceiling.
