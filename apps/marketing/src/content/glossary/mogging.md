## What does mogging mean

Mogging refers to the act of visually outcompeting someone in terms of physical attractiveness, typically in a side-by-side or direct social context. The person doing this is called the "mogger," and the one being outclassed is said to be "mogged." The term is used both seriously and humorously within looksmaxxing communities.

A "mogger skull" specifically refers to a facial bone structure considered dominant or high-status, typically characterized by a wide, forward-grown midface, strong jaw, prominent cheekbones, and deep-set eyes. The skull is treated as the foundation of facial attractiveness because soft tissue changes are limited compared to the underlying bone structure.

Mogging can be total (full-face dominance) or feature-specific. Someone might mog another person in jawline but not in eye area, for example. The concept is used analytically to break down which facial features carry the most visual weight in a given comparison.

## Origin

The term derives from "to mog," itself a verb coined in online looksmaxxing spaces, loosely adapted from older British slang and internet culture. It spread as a shorthand for describing appearance hierarchies in photos and real-life interactions, eventually spawning compound terms like "height mog," "frame mog," and "skull mog."
