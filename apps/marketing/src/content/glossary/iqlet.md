## What does iqlet mean

"Iqlet", also written "IQlet", is derogatory internet slang for a person considered very unintelligent or foolish. It works roughly like "idiot" or "moron", according to [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/iqlet). It is a put-down, not a description of anyone's measured intelligence.

In practice, people often aim it at a specific action or opinion rather than at a person in general. An [Urban Dictionary](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=iqlet) entry describes using it to call someone an idiot over something they did. Usage on TikTok stretches the meaning further. One aggregated [TikTok page](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/iqlet-meaning-slang) describes it as a label for someone perceived as having low intelligence or holding a belief others consider incorrect. The same page says it is often used to mock someone's views in online arguments. In those cases it can work as little more than a dismissive way of saying "I disagree with your take."

The available definitions are community-sourced and loose, so read the word in context. Beyond "stupid person", it has no settled meaning.

## Origin

Wiktionary gives the etymology as IQ plus the suffix "-let". The same pattern appears in "brainlet", which [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/brainlet) describes as a pejorative used mainly on 4chan for people with limited intelligence. Know Your Meme traces the earliest archived use of "brainlet" to the /sci/ board in December 2015. [Digital Cultures](https://digitalcultures.net/memes/brainlet/) notes that the "-let" works as a diminutive suffix.

Iqlet follows the same structure with "IQ" as the base. No source documents when or where iqlet itself was coined, so treat the brainlet link as a structural parallel rather than proven descent. The Urban Dictionary entry compares the two directly. It suggests brainlet is more of a picture, while iqlet describes an action.
