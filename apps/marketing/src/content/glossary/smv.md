## What does SMV mean

SMV stands for Sexual Market Value. It is a concept borrowed from evolutionary psychology and adapted by looksmaxxing communities to describe how attractive a person is likely to be perceived as a potential partner. The score is treated as a composite of physical traits such as facial structure, height, and body composition, along with social factors like status and confidence.

Within these communities, SMV is often rated on a scale of 1 to 10, with separate discussions about how different traits contribute to or detract from the overall score. It is not a clinical or scientific measurement. It is a rough framework people use to benchmark where they currently stand and identify what is realistically improvable.

SMV chadfishing refers to a specific test where someone uses photos of a high-SMV individual, typically a conventionally attractive man, to gauge responses on dating apps. The point is to compare outcomes and see how much appearance alone drives match rates and message quality. It highlights the outsized role physical attractiveness plays in initial dating app interactions.

## Origin

The term has roots in broader pickup artist and evolutionary psychology discourse, where the idea of a competitive "sexual marketplace" was used to explain dating dynamics. Looksmaxxing communities adopted and narrowed the concept to focus primarily on measurable physical traits, treating SMV improvement as a practical goal rather than a purely theoretical one.
