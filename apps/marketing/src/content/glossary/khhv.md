## What does KHHV mean

KHHV stands for Kissless Hugless Handholdless Virgin. Each letter adds a layer to describe someone with zero romantic physical experience: no kissing, no hugging in a romantic context, no hand-holding, and no sexual activity. It is an extension of the simpler term 'KHV' (Kissless Hugless Virgin), with the extra 'H' for hand-holding added to make the description more granular.

The term is used mostly as self-description in online communities focused on dating, attraction, and self-improvement. It functions as a shorthand for a specific social and romantic situation rather than a personality judgment. People use it to signal where they are starting from when discussing self-improvement goals.

## Origin

KHHV grew out of incel-adjacent and male self-improvement forums where users developed layered acronyms to categorize dating experience levels. The stacking of letters reflects a broader pattern in these spaces of creating precise social taxonomies. The term carries no formal definition outside these communities but is widely understood within them.
