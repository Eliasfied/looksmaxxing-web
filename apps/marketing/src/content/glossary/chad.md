## What does Chad mean

Chad describes a man who scores at the upper end of the PSL (Pretty Scale Lookism) rating system, generally an 8, 9, or 10 out of 10. The term is used to describe someone with strong facial structure, good skin, high cheekbones, a well-defined jaw, and hunter eyes, among other traits. In everyday usage, it has broadened to include social confidence and perceived dominance, though in looksmaxxing circles the focus stays mostly on physical features.

The chadlite vs chad distinction matters here. A chadlite sits just below the full Chad threshold, usually rated around a 7.5 to 8. He is clearly above average and does well socially, but lacks one or two key facial features that would push him into the top bracket. A true Chad tends to have near-universal appeal across different populations, which is why regional variations like the "Mexican Chad" or "Aero Chad" appear, each describing men who hit the highest tier within a specific ethnic or aesthetic subtype.

## Origin

The term originates from internet slang and was popularized in meme culture before being adopted by PSL communities. It started as a caricature of the socially and physically dominant male, contrasted against the "Virgin" archetype, as seen in comparisons like "virgin walk vs chad stride." Over time, looksmaxxing communities stripped away most of the satire and began using it as a practical rating category.
