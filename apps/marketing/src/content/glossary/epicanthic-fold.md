## What does epicanthic fold mean

An epicanthic fold is a fold of upper eyelid skin that partly covers the inner corner of the eye ([Dictionary.com](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/epicanthic-fold)). A surgical review describes it as a crescent-shaped extension of eyelid skin that can wholly or partly hide the lacrimal lake, the small pink area at the inner corner ([PMC review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12690353/)). It is also called an epicanthal fold or epicanthus.

Clinicians name types by where the fold starts, such as near the eyebrow (supraciliaris) or from the eyelid fold (tarsalis) ([PMC review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12690353/)). In epicanthus inversus, the fold runs upward from the lower lid instead ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/medicine/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/epicanthic-fold)).

It is a very common feature. A study cites an incidence between 40% and 90% in Asia ([PLOS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0187690)), and folds also appear in young children of any ancestry before the nasal bridge rises ([ADAM](https://sales-demo.adam.com/content.aspx?productid=117&pid=1&gid=003030)). Folds can be one feature of some genetic conditions, including Down syndrome ([Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/symptoms/22413-monolid-eyes)), but syndromes are diagnosed from a combination of features and testing, never from eye shape alone ([iSymptom](https://isymptom.com/symptom/epicanthal-folds/)). Cleveland Clinic advises calling your child's healthcare provider if you notice folds after the first well-baby checkup.

## Origin

"Epicanthus" means "above the canthus," from the Ancient Greek kanthos, corner of the eye ([FindZebra](https://www.findzebra.com/details/kL63P4V-epicanthic-fold)). Older texts used racialized synonyms such as "Mongoloid fold" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicanthic_fold)). These are outdated and offensive. The trait's exact evolutionary function and origin remain unknown ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicanthic_fold)).

## How it relates to looksmaxxing

In looksmaxxing spaces, the fold comes up in eye-area talk next to canthal tilt and eyelid crease. It is often confused with a monolid, but the terms describe different things. A monolid is an upper lid without a visible crease ([HandWiki](https://handwiki.org/wiki/Biology:Epicanthic_fold)), while the fold sits at the inner corner. The fold is a normal inherited trait, not a flaw to fix.
