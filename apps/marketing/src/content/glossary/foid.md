## What does foid mean

Foid is a slang abbreviation for 'female humanoid.' It is used in online male self-improvement communities, including looksmaxxing spaces, as a neutral-to-clinical shorthand for women. The term strips away social framing and treats attraction and mate selection as biological phenomena, which fits the pseudo-scientific tone common in these spaces.

The word carries different weight depending on context. Some users apply it descriptively and without hostility when discussing hypergamy, attraction theory, or dating dynamics. In other contexts, it can carry a dismissive or dehumanizing undertone. Understanding the term requires recognizing both usages without endorsing the more negative framing.

A 'hypergamous foid' refers to a woman who selects partners based on status, looks, or resources, specifically those equal to or above her own perceived value. This concept is frequently referenced when discussing why physical improvement and status building matter in the dating market.

## Origin

The term originated in incel-adjacent online communities and spread into broader looksmaxxing discourse from there. It was coined as a deliberately clinical-sounding label, borrowing the suffix '-oid' to frame human behavior through a detached, almost biological lens. Over time it migrated beyond its original subculture and is now used more loosely across self-improvement forums.
