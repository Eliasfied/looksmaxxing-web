## What does stacylite mean

In PSL and looksmaxxing communities, female attractiveness is often discussed using a tiered system. At the top sits the Stacy, a woman considered exceptionally attractive based on facial harmony, bone structure, and overall presentation. A stacylite sits one step below that, scoring high enough to be well above average but missing the specific traits, such as near-perfect facial symmetry or striking bone structure, that push someone into full Stacy territory.

Becky is a separate category used for average or near-average women. Becky is further broken down into low-tier Becky, mid-tier Becky, and high-tier Becky. A high-tier Becky approaches stacylite range but is still considered a distinct tier. The progression generally runs: low-tier Becky, mid-tier Becky, high-tier Becky, stacylite, Stacy. These labels are rough heuristics, not fixed scientific categories.

## Origin

The terms Stacy and Becky originated in online male-interest communities as shorthand for different female attractiveness levels, borrowing from common names as archetypes. Stacylite developed as a natural extension when users needed a word for women who scored high but did not fully reach Stacy-tier. The suffix "-lite" follows the same pattern seen in other PSL terms like normie-lite, indicating a close but not complete match to a given tier.
