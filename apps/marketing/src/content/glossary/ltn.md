## What does LTN mean

LTN stands for Low-Tier Normie. In looksmaxxing communities, attractiveness is often broken into tiers, and the normie range generally covers people who are neither conventionally attractive nor notably unattractive. An LTN sits at the lower end of that band, usually corresponding to a PSL score of roughly 4 to 5.

The term is descriptive, not a verdict. Most people placed in this category have clear room for improvement through soft and hard looksmaxxing. Factors like poor grooming, suboptimal body composition, weak posture, or an unflattering haircut can pull someone into LTN territory even if their underlying bone structure is decent. Addressing those variables can move someone meaningfully up the scale.

When someone asks what it means to "mog" in relation to LTN, it usually comes up in comparisons. An LTN getting mogged means a higher-tier individual is outcompeting them in a social or visual context. The flip side is that an LTN can still mog others below the normie range.

## Origin

The LTN label comes from the PSL rating system, which developed in online looksmaxxing spaces as a structured way to categorize male facial attractiveness. The tier system splits broadly into sub-5, normie (roughly 5 to 6), and above. Low-Tier Normie, Mid-Tier Normie (MTN), and High-Tier Normie (HTN) emerged to add more granularity within that middle band.

## How it relates to looksmaxxing

For someone who identifies or is rated as an LTN, looksmaxxing is directly relevant. The LTN range is widely considered the most responsive to softmaxxing interventions: fixing diet, training, skincare, style, and grooming can realistically push a person into MTN or even HTN territory without any medical procedures.

If you want an objective starting point, tools like the Aura app can give you a PSL score alongside specific analysis of features like jawline definition and eye area, helping you identify which variables are actually holding your rating down. That kind of targeted feedback makes improvement more efficient than guessing.
