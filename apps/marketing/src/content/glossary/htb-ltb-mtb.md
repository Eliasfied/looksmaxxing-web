## What do LTB, MTB and HTB mean

LTB stands for Low-Tier Becky, MTB for Mid-Tier Becky and HTB for High-Tier Becky. "Becky" is forum slang for an average woman, so the three tiers split the broad middle of the female looks scale: LTB is below average, MTB is roughly average and HTB is clearly above average. Above HTB sits "Stacy", the slang for the top tier.

The tiers mirror the male scale. LTB pairs with [LTN](/glossary/ltn) (Low-Tier Normie), MTB with [MTN](/glossary/mtn) and HTB with [HTN](/glossary/htn). In rating threads the labels are often attached to rough PSL-style numbers, but there is no fixed cut-off, and two people rating the same photo regularly land a tier apart.

A second, much smaller usage shows up in TikTok self-improvement videos, where HTB is sometimes read as "high-tier bloomer": someone whose looks improved a lot over time. On looksmaxxing forums and in most comment sections, Becky is the meaning.

## Origin

The terms grew out of incel and looksmaxxing forums, where "Stacy" and "Becky" have long served as stock names for very attractive and average women, the female counterparts to "Chad" and "normie". Adding low, mid and high tiers to Becky made the slang as fine-grained as the normie tiers used for men. The short forms then spread to Reddit and TikTok through rating threads and comment sections.

## How it relates to looksmaxxing

The tiers are rating slang, not a measurement. They squeeze looks into three boxes and leave out what changes how someone comes across in real life: skin, body composition, hair, style, and how a photo was taken. A label from a forum thread says more about the thread than about the person, and the same caution applies to the male tiers.
