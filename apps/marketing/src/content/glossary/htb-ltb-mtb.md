## What do HTB, LTB, and MTB mean

HTB stands for High-Tier Bloomer, LTB for Low-Tier Bloomer, and MTB for Mid-Tier Bloomer. The "bloomer" label refers to someone whose looks develop positively over time, whether through puberty, lifestyle changes, or deliberate self-improvement. The tier describes how dramatic that transformation is or is expected to be.

A High-Tier Bloomer (HTB) is someone who started below their potential and eventually reached an objectively attractive result. A Mid-Tier Bloomer (MTB) lands at an average or above-average outcome after improving. A Low-Tier Bloomer (LTB) shows some improvement but remains below average in conventional attractiveness metrics. The terms are descriptive rather than fixed, since lifestyle, training, and other factors can shift someone between categories.

These labels are distinct from pure rating scales like PSL scores because they emphasize change over time rather than a static snapshot. Someone can be rated low at baseline but still be classified as a potential HTB if the underlying structure suggests strong upside.

## Origin

The bloomer framework grew out of online self-improvement communities focused on male attractiveness. It spread onto TikTok and short-form video content where before-and-after transformations became a common format. The HTB and LTB shorthand made it easier to tag or caption those videos quickly.
