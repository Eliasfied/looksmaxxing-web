## What does IQmax mean

IQmax, short for IQ maximization, describes intentional efforts to improve cognitive performance. This includes habits and interventions targeting memory, focus, processing speed, and general mental sharpness. Common methods associated with iqmaxxing include sleep optimization, strength training, dietary changes, reading, learning new skills, and in some cases nootropic supplementation.

The term follows the same naming pattern as other maxxing concepts: take a trait, add "maxx" or "maxxing," and you have a shorthand for systematically pursuing it. IQmax is sometimes called brainmaxxing, with both terms used interchangeably in self-improvement communities. The goal is not necessarily to raise IQ scores in a clinical sense, but to perform closer to your cognitive ceiling through lifestyle choices.

Research suggests that factors like consistent sleep, cardiovascular exercise, and reduced processed food intake may help support cognitive function. Some users report improved mental clarity after addressing basic deficiencies in areas like sleep quality and physical activity before adding more complex interventions.

## Origin

The term grew out of the broader "maxxing" vocabulary that emerged from online male self-improvement spaces. As communities expanded beyond purely physical improvements like jawline development or body composition, cognitive performance became another axis to optimize. IQmax and brainmaxxing became shorthand for that cognitive dimension of the self-improvement stack.

## How it relates to looksmaxxing

At first glance, cognitive performance and physical appearance seem separate. In practice, many looksmaxxers treat them as overlapping. Behaviors that support IQmaxxing, such as quality sleep, low body fat, and stress management, also directly affect skin health, hormonal balance, and overall physical presentation. Chronic sleep deprivation, for example, visibly affects under-eye appearance and skin texture.

IQmax also matters for executing a looksmaxxing plan effectively. Tracking progress, researching treatments, interpreting feedback from tools like the Aura app, and staying consistent over time all require focus and discipline. Some users approach their Aura PSL scores and personalized improvement plans analytically, treating looksmaxxing itself as a cognitive exercise.
